Before making an offer

Have the broker sanity-check mortgage compatibility, but remember the bank's own valuation controls the financeable base. Ask the agent for APE, planimetria, rendita catastale, condominium statement/minutes and known extraordinary works before committing. Use a properly drafted mortgage/finance condition in the proposal if advised by your notary/broker.

How the model calculates

Mortgage: LTV × the lower of agreed price and ING valuation, capped by the editable ING pre-approval ceiling. The model retains the original 95% input ceiling for comparison; this is not confirmation of an available LTV for your transaction. Confirm applicable limits and approval with ING.

Private seller + prima casa: price-value model uses rendita catastale × 1.05 × 110, with 2% registration tax (minimum €1,000) plus €50 mortgage tax + €50 cadastral tax. VAT-subject company + prima casa: 4% VAT on purchase price + €200 + €200 + €200 fixed taxes. Non-prima-casa comparison: private/prezzo-valore uses ×120 and 9% (minimum €1,000) + €100; VAT uses 10% + €600; mortgage substitute tax is 2%. This simplified model excludes luxury-category VAT, special exemptions and transactions ineligible for prezzo-valore. Have the notary verify applicability. Notary/technical entries should include VAT and expenses but exclude taxes already listed, to avoid double counting.

Important: This is a planning tool, not a bank offer, tax opinion, valuation or notarial quote. Agency/notary/technical fees are editable estimates. Confirm the actual transaction structure, prima-casa eligibility, marital-property treatment, taxes and mortgage conditions with your broker, ING and notary before signing a proposta or preliminare.
